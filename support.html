Support

Something not working, or an idea for the game? Write to thinhle.support@gmail.com. Please include your device model, its system version and the app version (shown in the store listing), and the level if it is about one (for example “4-Piece · Level 14”, shown at the top of the play screen).

How to play

  • Drag a piece from beside the silhouette onto it.
  • Tap a piece to turn it 45°. The Rotate and Flip buttons in the bottom-right corner act on the piece you touched last (it has a light outline).
  • Pieces click onto the edges of the silhouette and of pieces already placed. Fill the whole silhouette to solve the level; any arrangement that fills it counts.

Questions

A piece won’t click into place.

Pieces only snap when their edge is parallel to the edge they should meet. Turn or flip the piece first, then drop it close to the spot.

Can I change or hide the pet in the corner?

Yes. In Settings > Companion you can pick a cat or a dog, its look and coat, and the corner it sits in, or turn it off.

How do I get more coins?

Solve a level for the first time (more if you used no hint), finish a chapter, check in every day, or watch an optional rewarded ad from the Shop.

I bought “Remove Ads”, but I see ads on my new device.

Open Settings > Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. Rewarded ads (the ones you choose to watch for coins) stay available after “Remove Ads”.

Will I lose my progress or coins if I change phones?

Your progress, coins and themes sync through iCloud on iPhone and iPad, and through Google Play Games on Android, when you use the same account on both devices. On Android, sign in to Play Games (Settings > Sign in to Play Games appears when you are signed out). Sync works between devices of the same platform only; moving between iOS and Android is not possible.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Every level works without a connection. Ads, purchases and cloud sync need the internet and catch up when you are back online.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the option applies (for example in the EEA and the UK), use Settings > Privacy options. On iOS, tracking permission is in the system Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See the privacy policy for details.

A purchase failed or I was charged without getting coins.

Purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play. If the payment went through but nothing arrived, restart the app with an internet connection; pending purchases are delivered then. If it still does not arrive, email us with the date and the order number from the store receipt. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (your order history in the Play Store).
